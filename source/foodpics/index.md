# Foodpics
I love cooking and even though I'm certainly no master chef, I still want to share my creations here 🧑‍🍳. Most of the dishes I cook are rather simple. Many of them are improvisational, some follow a recipe (which I usually still tweak here and there ...) and sometimes, on special occasions, they also become a bit extravagant.

Since I eat a vegetarian diet, all meals are either veggie or vegan, while I try to cook as balanced as possible - oftens times with a particular focus on including a decent amount of proteins.

For the most part, I'll just post pictures and meal titles, no precise recipes. Feel free to hit me up though if you're curious about one of the dishes.
